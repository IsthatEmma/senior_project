Mindful Monday is a activity that helps students in class to help start the week in a calm way.

It often includes short reflections, breathing exercises or discussions about things like stress, kindness and staying positive.

The goal is to help students slow down and clear their minds and be more aware of their feelings & thoughts.

It also helps create a feel more relaxed and ready to learn for the week!
